Disclaimer
Bodycraft is a personal tracking and reminder tool. Its routines, meal ideas, calorie numbers, goal weights and AI feedback are general information only. They are not medical, nutrition or exercise advice, and they don't replace the judgment of a doctor, registered dietitian or other professional. Calorie estimates from photos are rough guesses by an AI model and can be far off. Use them as a reference, not as exact numbers. If you have a health condition, take medication, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are under 18, or feel strong anxiety or a loss of control around food or weight, please talk with a health professional before using the plan features.
A slower, steadier pace of fat loss is easier to sustain. Bodycraft will flag goals that are clearly too fast, but you remain responsible for your own health.
